Instrumental only, absolutely no vocals or singing. About 64 seconds long.
A gentle, whimsical, handmade-feeling score for a paper-collage animated short film about how the brain creates dreams during sleep.
Key of F major, about 76 BPM, soft and intimate, warm analog room sound, like a small chamber ensemble recorded close-up.
Instruments: music box and celesta melody, soft felt piano, plucked pizzicato strings, warm muted string pad, light harp arpeggios, a little glockenspiel sparkle, very soft brushed percussion only in the middle.
Arc: begins hushed and nocturnal with a lone music box lullaby; then curious and playful as felt piano and pizzicato join (a sense of wonder, a little mischievous); around the middle it blooms into a dreamy, floating swell with harp glissando and strings, like drifting on a sea of dreams; then settles into a calm, reflective passage; then turns warm and bright like morning sunlight; ends softly on a sustained major chord with a final celesta chime and a natural fade.
Cozy, tasteful, cinematic but understated, in the spirit of an independent animated film score. Mixed to sit underneath a narrator: no loud drums, no big brass, no sudden hits.
